Before confirming an ambulance, ask about the vehicle type, oxygen and medical equipment, accompanying medical staff if required, estimated travel time, route, waiting charges, tolls and total fare. Keep the patient's medical summary and treating doctor's transfer advice ready. For a medical emergency requiring immediate help, contact local emergency services without delay.
